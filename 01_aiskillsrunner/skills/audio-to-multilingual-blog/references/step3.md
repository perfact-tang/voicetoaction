# 中文定稿 → English blog

审定后的 `中文.md` 是内容基准。直接从中文翻译为自然、可发表的英文；不要从日文转译，也不要从 STT 另写一篇。

- Preserve the argument, evidence, narrator, facts, numbers, named entities, causality, negation, qualifications, and degree of certainty. Add no English-only claims or conclusions.
- Use idiomatic English appropriate to the topic and the author's voice. Recast sentence syntax, explicit subjects, transitions, and paragraph breaks as needed. Do not assume every source is a technical blog.
- Translate Chinese slang, internet expressions, metaphors, and wordplay by their meaning, tone, and intensity in context. Use an idiomatic equivalent when it fits; otherwise express the meaning plainly. Avoid literal calques, forced slang, and newly invented cultural references or examples.
- Contextual examples: “踩坑” may mean “run into pitfalls” or “learn the hard way”; “太水了” about an article may mean “too much fluff” or “thin on substance”; “内卷” may mean “a race to the bottom” or “cutthroat competition.” Choose by context, not by a fixed replacement list.
- Preserve section order and Markdown hierarchy, lists, links, and code. Localize headings and prose naturally; split or combine ordinary paragraphs when it improves English flow. Keep code, URLs, and identifiers unchanged.
- Read the translation independently for naturalness, then compare it section by section with the Chinese manuscript. Correct omissions, additions, changes in stance or certainty, and errors in negation or conditions. Review slang for both meaning and register.

Write only the reviewed article to `英文.md`, with no preamble or translator commentary.
